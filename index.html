2025–2026

CAAM-Net: Conflict-Aware Multimodal Bangla Clickbait Detection

Undergraduate thesis research · Manuscript submitted

Developed a conflict-aware multimodal model using BanglaBERT, ConvNeXtV2-Tiny, and bidirectional cross-attention. Evaluated with 8,108 validated title–thumbnail pairs, 11 baselines, and channel-disjoint testing.

Research overview ↗

2025

Bangla Hate Speech Detection: Transformer Ensembles & Multi-task Learning

Research leading to BLP-2025 publication

Developed transformer ensemble strategies and a shared-encoder multi-task model to classify Bangla hate speech by type, target, and severity, with comparative experiments and error analysis.
